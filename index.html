About Lavender

A little warmth in every bite.

Your Home Away From Home

Lavender is a cozy bakery and café that serves fresh, scratch-made pastries, artisan cakes, desserts, handcrafted coffees, creamy lattes, refreshing boba drinks, and more.

We believe everyone deserves a peaceful place to enjoy delicious food, meaningful conversations, or even quiet moments alone. Every recipe is prepared with care to make each visit feel comforting and memorable.

💜 The Problem We Solve

Modern life can be busy and stressful. Lavender offers a relaxing space with high-quality baked goods, comforting drinks, and exceptional hospitality so every guest can slow down and enjoy life's little moments.

    Good to Know

    Quick answers to common questions.

    Do you do custom celebration cakes?

    Yes! Message us a few days ahead with your flavour, size and date and we'll bake it fresh.

    Can I study or work at Lavender?

    Absolutely. Many of our guests settle in with a latte and a laptop — it's one of our favourite things.

    Do you offer takeaway?

    Every item can be packed to go. Use the cart on this page and send your order straight to us on WhatsApp.

    Are there vegetarian options?

    Plenty — ask our team and we'll point you to the best picks.
